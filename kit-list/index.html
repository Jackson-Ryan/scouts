    How it works

    What you get

    Built around your trip

    Quantities adjust to the number of nights, weather and section. Extra questions only appear for activities you pick.

    Print-ready PDF

    Generates an A4 PDF in your browser with tick boxes, quantities, notes and space for leader contact details.

    Spreadsheet & OSM CSV

    Exports a clean CSV with Item, Category, Quantity, Notes columns so you can paste straight into Online Scout Manager.

    Using the CSV with Online Scout Manager: OSM does not have a public CSV import for checklists, so the file is formatted to open in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets and copy straight into OSM’s checklist screen.
