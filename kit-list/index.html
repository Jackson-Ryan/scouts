    How it works

    Less time packing, more time adventuring

    Answer the questions

    Section, trip type, weather, accommodation and activities. Conditional questions appear only when they matter.

    Get a tailored list

    Quantities change with the number of nights, the season and the activities. Beavers get a simpler list than Scouts.

    Download and share

    A print-ready PDF for families, plus a CSV with category, item, quantity and notes for your OSM checklist.

    About the CSV: Online Scout Manager's public checklist tools do not include a documented bulk-import file, so the CSV is laid out to open in a spreadsheet and copy straight into OSM's checklist screen — or into any checklist tool that accepts a CSV. The columns are Item, Category, Quantity, Notes.
