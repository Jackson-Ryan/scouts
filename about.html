About Scout Tools

A small set of free, independent tools for Beaver, Cub and Scout leaders. No accounts, no adverts, and everything runs in your browser.

Scout Tools brings practical planning helpers together in one place so you can pick a game or put together a camp kit list without digging through old spreadsheets and PDFs.

How the tools work

Everything runs locally in your browser. Saved games and kit list answers stay on your own device in localStorage, and no personal details about young people are collected or uploaded.

Games Finder

Filter 50 games by section, length, theme and setting. Open any game for equipment, set-up steps, rules, safety notes and adaptations.

Kit List Generator

Step through trip details, weather and activities to build a packing list. Export a printable PDF for parents or a CSV for Online Scout Manager.

Saved in your browser

Bookmark games or pause halfway through a kit list and come back later. Nothing requires a login.

Built to print

Game sheets and kit lists print cleanly without menus or clutter, ready for a leader folder or kit table.

Variants vs themed twists

In the Games Finder, variants and themed twists are kept separate so you can tell at a glance whether the rules have changed.

Variant

Different rules

A variant is its own entry in the library with updated equipment, rules and safety notes. Snowball Siege is a variant of French v English because the throwing and scoring change.

Themed twist

Same rules, new story

A themed twist changes the props or story (pumpkins instead of balls, moon bases instead of chairs) while the rules stay the same. Spider Football is a twist on Crab Football, not a separate game.

When adding a game, link it as a variant only when the rules change. For a seasonal or themed re-skin, add it as a themed twist instead.

Safety notes

Your own risk assessment still comes first

Safety notes and kit lists here are a starting point, not a substitute for your own risk assessment, venue checks, safeguarding procedures or Nights Away guidance.

Check the space, equipment and individual needs of your group before playing a game or sending out a kit list. Adapt anything that does not suit your venue, and stop any game that starts to feel unsafe.

Adding a game or suggesting a tool

Use the add a game form to write up a game, preview its card and submit it straight from the app — no GitHub account needed. Submitted games go into a quick review queue before appearing in the library.

Please include practical safety notes and adaptations, write the rules in your own words, and credit the source if a game came from elsewhere. If you have an idea for another tool, open an issue on GitHub.

Run your own copy

Scout Tools is a plain static site built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no database or server to maintain. Games live in games/data/games.json and the home page reads tools.json. You can fork the GitHub repository and publish your own copy with GitHub Pages.

Setup instructions, the game schema and validation scripts are documented in the repository README.md, games/README.md and kit-list/README.md.
